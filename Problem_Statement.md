✈️ Flight Price Comparison & Booking Platform (Skyscanner-style)






   Problem Statement:

* Many travelers visit different airline and travel websites to compare flight prices, schedules, and availability before booking a ticket. This process takes a lot of time and makes it difficult to find the best flight at the lowest price.

* The Flight Price Comparison & Booking Platform (Skyscanner-style) solves this problem by allowing users to search, compare, and book flights from different airlines in one place. The platform helps users choose the best flight based on price, timing, and airline, making the booking process faster, easier, and more convenient.
